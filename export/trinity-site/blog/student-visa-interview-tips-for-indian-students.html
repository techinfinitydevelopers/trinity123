A visa interview is less about your grades and more about three things: genuine intent to study, ability to pay, and intent to comply with visa rules. Every question below tests one of those.

Why the interview matters

Officers spend only a few minutes per applicant. Clear, consistent, confident answers backed by documents win; memorised speeches and vague finances lose.

The 12 questions

  1. Why did you choose this university?
  2. Why this course, and how does it connect to your background?
  3. Why not study this course in India?
  4. Who is sponsoring your education?
  5. What is your sponsor’s annual income and occupation?
  6. Have you taken an education loan? From which bank?
  7. What are your plans after graduation?
  8. Do you have relatives in the destination country?
  9. What was your test score (IELTS / TOEFL / GRE)?
  10. How many universities did you apply to, and where else were you accepted?
  11. Where will you stay?
  12. What do you know about the city / campus?

Documents to carry

  • Offer / acceptance letter and fee receipts
  • Passport and appointment confirmation
  • Financial proof: bank statements, loan sanction letter, sponsor ITRs
  • Academic transcripts and test score reports
  • Statement of Purpose copy

Mistakes that cause refusals

Inconsistent finances, unclear course-to-career logic, over-rehearsed answers and hinting at permanent settlement are the top four. Our mock interviews target exactly these gaps before the real appointment.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a student visa interview?

Usually 3–5 minutes for the USA; UK, Canada and Ireland decisions are largely document-based with interviews only in specific cases.
